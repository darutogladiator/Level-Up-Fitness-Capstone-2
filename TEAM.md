## Project Summary

Level Up Fitness was developed partially during Capstone 1 and is now continuing as a full-stack AI fitness tracker. The goal is to make fitness more engaging by turning workouts and consistency into an RPG-style progression system with quests, XP, ranks, levels, and AI-assisted recommendations.
